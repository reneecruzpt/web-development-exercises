RGB color model

RGB (abbreviation of the English words red, green, blue - red, green, blue) is a color model that describes the way color is coded in electronic devices. In the RGB model, three colors (often referred to as the primary colors) are used for color reproduction:

  • Red
  • Green
  • Blue

The choice of these colors is related to the structural features of the human retina. In the retina, there are 3 types of cones that are receptors responsible for the perception of color. The receptor of each type perceives only one of the three colors of the RGB model.
