Do you know why the blue color palette is used in the design of Facebook? Mark Zuckerberg suffers from color blindness. He does not distinguish between red and green.

The orange color is named after ripe oranges. Before this word was introduced into the English-speaking world, orange was called yellowish-red.

The real color of the Sun is white. It can be seen by looking at the sun from space. Our atmosphere, refracting the sun's rays, makes it yellowish.

The most common color for markers is yellow. Why? It leaves no shadow when photocopying.

Colorblind people have better night vision than people with normal color perception.
