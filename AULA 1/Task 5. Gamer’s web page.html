Pac-Man

General information

Pac-Man (Pac-Man) Pac-Man is an arcade video game developed in 1980 by the Japanese company Namco. The player's task is to control Pac-Man to eat all the points in the maze and avoid meeting the ghosts that are chasing the character.

Game characters

The main character, Pac-Man, was made to be friendly, so people could associate themselves with him. The role of enemies in Pac-Man is played by four colorful ghosts:

  • Blinky: the red ghost stalking Pacman without much fuss.
  • Pinky: the pink ghost that targets a position 32 pixels from in front of the protagonist.
  • Inky: the blue ghost trying to hit a point that takes into account the position of the following objects:
    1. the player;
    2. another ghost - Blinky.
  • Clyde: the orange ghost that, depending on its distance from the main character, either chases after Pac-Man or goes to the lower-left corner of the maze.

Level 256

As conceived by the developers, it was impossible to get to the end of Pac-Man, as the complexity of the game increased from level to level. However, players found that starting from level 21, the behavior of the ghosts became the same. This made it possible to build a "winning" route along which the strongest players were able to get to level 256.

In level 265, a part of the maze is displayed incorrectly. It turned out that this was the consequence of a software error, the existence of which the game developers did not even suspect until the American Billy Mitchell passed the first 255 levels and pointed out the problem.
